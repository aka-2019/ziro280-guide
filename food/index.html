On foot · one stop

DDP · Gwangjang Market · Euljiro

Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP) DDP · Design Store

🚶 3 min · 200 m

🕒 Daily 10:00–20:00 · outdoor plaza always open

Zaha Hadid's curved landmark. Exhibitions, markets and pop-ups run all year, and the illuminated facade is at its best after dark.

  • Pick up design souvenirs at the DDP Design Store, B2 of the Eoullim Plaza
  • Some exhibitions are free, some ticketed — check the sign at the entrance

Gwangjang Market 광장시장

🚶 24 min · 1.5 km

🕒 Stalls vary · roughly late morning to about 21:00

A century-old covered market famous for mung-bean pancakes (bindaetteok), raw beef (yukhoe) and knife-cut noodles. Tables line the narrow aisles; upstairs is vintage clothing and hanbok.

  • Soonhine Bindaetteok 10:00–21:00 · Buchon Yukhoe 10:00–21:00
  • A few stalls take cash only — carry a little cash
  • Weekend lunch queues are long; arriving before 11:00 helps

Euljiro Nogari Alley · Manseon Hof 을지로 노가리골목

🚇 Line 2, 2 stops · or 27 min walk

🕒 Evening to around midnight · Manseon Hof 12:00–24:00

Old-school beer houses in the alleys of Euljiro 3-ga. Dried fish and draft beer — the liveliest night street in Seoul right now.

  • 3 min from Euljiro 3-ga Stn (Line 2); very busy after 19:00
  • Seating is tight and some spots are standing-only

Coffee Hanyakbang 커피한약방

🚇 4 min from Euljiro 3-ga Stn

🕒 Mon–Fri 10:00–22:00 · Sat 11:00–22:00 · Sun 11:00–20:00

A cafe tucked in an alley inside a former herbal-medicine shop. A good daytime rest stop while exploring Euljiro.

  • The sign is small and set back in the alley — follow the map pin

Dongdaemun Dakhanmari Alley 동대문 닭한마리 골목

🚶 16 min · 1.0 km

🕒 Jinokhwa Halmae 10:30–01:00

A whole chicken simmered in broth, eaten with noodles — 'dakhanmari'. One pot serves two to three; finish with a portion of knife-cut noodles.

  • Jinokhwa Halmae is the best-known shop; expect a short wait

Woo Lae Oak 우래옥

🚶 18 min · 1.2 km

🕒 11:30–21:00 · closed Mondays

A Pyongyang-style cold noodle institution since 1946. Mild, clean broth and grilled bulgogi — a gentle first try for cold noodles.

  • Queues at lunch · closed on Mondays

Myeongdong Kyoja 명동교자

🚇 Line 4, 2 stops · about 10 min

🕒 Daily 10:30–21:00

Since 1966 this shop has served just a handful of dishes: rich chicken-broth kalguksu and large steamed dumplings.

Myeongdong street · money exchange 명동 거리

🚇 Line 4, 2 stops

🕒 Most shops 10:00–21:00

Seoul's busiest shopping street for cosmetics, fashion and snacks. Private exchange booths here usually beat bank rates.

  • Bring your passport for currency exchange
  • Fewer shops open after 21:00

Myeongdong Cathedral 명동성당

🚇 5 min from Myeongdong Stn

🕒 06:30–19:30 · Sunday until 22:00

A red-brick Gothic cathedral from 1898. Entry is free and the steps in front are a favourite photo spot.

  • Please stay quiet and pause your visit during mass

Namdaemun Market · braised hairtail alley 남대문시장

🚇 Line 4, 3 stops · Hoehyeon Stn

🕒 Shops 09:00–17:00 · hairtail alley 10:00–22:00

A 600-year-old market: braised hairtail (galchi-jorim) alley, hotteok and noodle stalls, plus glasses, bags and souvenirs.

  • Many shops close on Sundays — check before a Sunday visit
  • Cash is easier for small purchases

Getting around

From DDP, and to the airport

Subway 지하철

📍 Lines 2 · 4 · 5

💳 Base fare 1,550 KRW (transit card) · 1,650 KRW single ticket

The nearest station is Dongdaemun History & Culture Park, served by Line 2 (Seongsu, Hongdae), Line 4 (Myeongdong, Seoul Stn) and Line 5.

  • Buy and top up a transit card at any convenience store
  • Last-train times differ by line and weekday — check a map app

Airport Bus 6001 공항버스 6001

🚌 DDP stop, in front

🕒 First 04:36 · last 19:56 (Nov 2026) · 17,000 KRW

A limousine bus straight to Incheon Airport. From the Dongdaemun DDP stop it takes about 70–90 minutes.

  • No reservation — first come, first served; pay cash or with a transit card
  • Coming from the airport, the stop order differs and some city stops are skipped
  • The last bus is early; for late-afternoon departures the airport train is safer

AREX airport railroad 공항철도

🚇 Seoul Stn · Hongik Univ. Stn

🎫 All-stop 4,750 KRW (Seoul Stn→T1) · express 13,000 KRW, 43 min

Take Line 4 to Seoul Station (4 stops) and change to the airport railroad. You can also board at Hongik Univ. Station.

  • The express train starts at Seoul Station only, with assigned seats and a separate ticket
  • All-stop trains accept transit cards; Hongik Univ. Stn to Incheon Airport takes about 65 min

Taxi & late night 택시

🌙 After the last train

📱 Hail with the Kakao T or Uber app

Taxis are easy to find in front of DDP and on the main road. At night, an app ride is simplest — you don't need to explain the address in Korean.

  • Showing the driver the address on your phone is the surest way
  • Night buses are limited — check the route in an app first

Good to know 현지 팁

💡

Water and side dishes are free in restaurants, and tipping is not expected. Pay at the counter on your way out.

  • Cards work almost everywhere, but cash is easier at market stalls
  • For cafe Wi-Fi, just ask staff for the password
We recommend checking opening hours in a map app before you set out — many places close on Sundays and public holidays.
